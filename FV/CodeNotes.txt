智能调整行高|GN001|一键调整当前工作表已用区域的行高，支持混合换行|Sub 智能调整行高()\n    Dim rng As Range\n    Dim cell As Range\n    \n    Set rng = ActiveSheet.UsedRange\n    rng.Rows.AutoFit\n    \n    For Each cell In rng\n        If cell.WrapText = True Then\n            cell.RowHeight = cell.RowHeight * 1.5\n        End If\n    Next cell\nEnd Sub
